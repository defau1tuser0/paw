# Context: Paw

Glossary of domain terms and their precise definitions.

## Glossary

### Case
An active record of an animal in need of rescue or care, initiated by a user capturing a photo and location. A Case goes through a lifecycle of status changes as the community coordinates to help the animal.

### Case Status
The current phase of a Case:
- **Open**: The Case has been reported and is awaiting community attention or action.
- **In Progress**: A rescuer or volunteer is actively responding to the Case (e.g., traveling to the location, searching, or transporting the animal).
- **Resolved**: The animal has been successfully rescued, treated, or placed in a safe environment.

### User
Any registered account on the platform. By default, a User can report Cases, comment on Cases, and update Case statuses.

### NGO
A specialized profile for a Non-Governmental Organization. NGOs have public profiles, coordinate larger rescue operations, and appear on the Map.

### Vet Clinic
A specialized profile representing a veterinary clinic. Vet Clinics display operating hours, contact information, services, and locations on the Map.

### Comment
A public message or photo attached to a Case's timeline. Comments are used to coordinate the rescue, share updates, and document progress.

### Direct Message (DM)
A private chat message sent directly between two Users.

### Friendship
A mutual connection established between two Users, enabling them to easily communicate via Direct Messages.

### Location
The geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude) and text description representing where a Case was reported, or where a Vet Clinic or NGO is situated.

### Map
An interactive visual display showing nearby Vet Clinics, NGOs, and active Cases, prioritizing veterinary and rescue assistance facilities based on the user's current or selected location.
